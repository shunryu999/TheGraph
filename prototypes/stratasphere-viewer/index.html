How to read it

Each shell is the Earth at one moment. The present is the outer shell, and every earlier moment is nested inside it. Each thing appears on the shell of the moment it began, at the place it began, and a radial line carries it outward to the present, or stops where it ended. A link runs from where it came from, curving inward through time and across the globe.

Why this dataset

Record and inference

Links drawn in grey and names in italic are inference, not record. Each dataset says in its notes (shown on the card when nothing is picked) what was reconstructed and why. Keeping inference visibly distinct from record is the first rule of the instrument.

The format

{"meta":{"title","unit","tStart","tEnd"}, "nodes":[{"id","label","lat","lng","t","tEnd","provenance"}], "links":[{"from","to","kind","inferred"}]}. Dates may be years, months, days or years before present. Any lineage with places and dates can be loaded the same way: species, manuscripts, editions, software forks. The full format is in data-format.md.
