    What this first version represents

    Life opens biological records; Mind opens human-made lineages. Energy and Matter await suitable datasets. “I” adds your optional viewing position without inventing a personal ancestry. The loops follow the existing Ontograph plates; this interface does not settle the open interpretation of their pinch point.

    Each shell is a dated event in the selected dataset. The spatial pane shows records whose stated intervals intersect that moment; an occurrence is shown at its own dated shell. Empty shells are labeled explicitly. Expanding a shell changes the displayed time extent, not the source data or geography.
